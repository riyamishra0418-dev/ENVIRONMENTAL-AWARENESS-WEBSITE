WHY IT MATTERS

Our Planet Needs Us

Environmental awareness helps us understand how our choices affect the Earth—and how we can make a difference.

🌳

Protect Nature

Care for forests, conserve water and protect the habitats that wildlife depends on.

♻️

Reduce Waste

Choose reusable products, sort waste correctly and avoid unnecessary single-use items.

💚

Live Sustainably

Save energy, make thoughtful purchases and build habits that are kinder to the planet.

LIVE A LITTLE GREENER

Eco-Friendly Habits

You don't have to change everything overnight. Start with one practical habit and keep going.

💧

Save Water

Turn off taps when not in use and report leaks when you can.

🚲

Travel Thoughtfully

Walk, cycle, carpool or use public transport when practical.

🌱

Care for Green Spaces

Look after local plants and join responsible community clean-ups.

🔌

Save Energy

Switch off lights and devices when you don't need them.

🛍️

Bring Reusables

Carry a reusable bag or bottle when it suits your day.

🥕

Waste Less Food

Plan meals, store food well and compost suitable scraps where possible.

THINK BEFORE YOU THROW

Know Your Waste

Sorting waste can make reuse, recycling and safe disposal easier. Follow the rules of your local waste collection service.

🍌

Wet / Organic Waste

Fruit and vegetable scraps and other suitable food waste. Compost them where facilities are available.

TIP: KEEP COMPOSTABLES SEPARATE
📦

Dry / Recyclable Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, metal and accepted plastic packaging. Keep recyclable materials dry and clean.

TIP: CHECK LOCAL RULES
🔋

Special Waste

Batteries, electronics and hazardous products may need designated collection or disposal points.

TIP: NEVER BURN E-WASTE
